Calculation and limits

  • Priority order is the desk author synthesis, not a published consensus ranking.
  • Illustration assumes starting budget and remaining budget both equal $100. Unspecified-base recommendations are treated as full-budget reference figures for this calculation; that is an assumption, not a source fact.
  • Use each expert publisher once, base tier only; exclude community bids and separate superflex or larger-league variants.
  • For finite intervals, take the midpoint, then take the median across publishers and round half up to the nearest whole percent. Dollar bids are normalized by their stated $100 reference.
  • One-sided ceilings or floors are retained as context but excluded from the median; a lone finite bid is labeled single source.
  • No numeric estimate where no verified publisher bid exists. A computed median is not a predicted winning bid or proof that experts agree.
